OrganelleScope

A local desktop microscopy workbench combining the Probabilistic Colocalization Estimator (PCE) and Quick Immunofluorescence Image Line Profiler (QILP). It connects calibrated image inspection, ROI measurements, colocalization, 3D analysis and traceable biological interpretation in one workflow.

The research problem

Microscopy analysis involves more than a single coefficient or line profile. Researchers need to inspect the source image, check calibration and channel identities, choose ROIs, compare measurements and retain the settings behind each result. OrganelleScope brings these steps into a local workbench for macOS and Windows.

My contribution

I defined the microscopy use cases and analysis workflows and developed the workbench with AI-assisted coding. The project extends my biomedical imaging experience while I continue to develop programming and AI skills through my Master of Artificial Intelligence.

analysis workflow
TIFF / OME-TIFF→Channels & calibration→ROI / 3D measurements→Review & interpretation→Traceable export

Image analysis

  • Native 8/16-bit TIFF data, static images and Z stacks with up to four channels.
  • Calibrated circular and polygonal ROIs, line profiles and FWHM measurements.
  • Defined Pearson, Spearman and Manders measurements, background subtraction and optional Gaussian smoothing.
  • Full-resolution 3D cuboids, threshold masks, calibrated voxel-centre proximity and radial enrichment.
  • ROI, threshold and cuboid sensitivity sweeps.

Review and reproducibility

  • Reviewed per-image batches and Excel/JSON exports.
  • Experiment-level resampling with explicit replication assumptions.
  • Portable projects, recovery state and analysis-review reports with embedded ROI previews.
  • Optional local Gemma assistant for human-reviewed drafts, with source-support and question-coverage checks.

Measurements depend on acquisition quality, calibration, controls and experimental design. Image overlap and rendered proximity do not establish molecular binding.

Project history

Two focused tools, one workbench.

OrganelleScope combines PCE and QILP. PCE is the predecessor and migration source; QILP contributed profiling helpers, types, charting, TIFF chart export and selected tests, with adaptations for the integrated workbench. Their original pages remain available below.
