01 / CHARACTER PROFILE

One career, several useful angles.

My education began in medical biotechnology, moved into health research ethics and worked in biomedical research, and became increasingly quantitative through microscopy and image analysis.

At HKUST(GZ), I worked on protein localization, kinase signalling and microtubule nucleation using confocal and super-resolution microscopy. Alongside that research, I began turning recurring analysis problems into small scientific software and automation projects.

I am currently pursuing a Master of Artificial Intelligence at Monash University and participating in GCI World 2026 at the Matsuo-Iwasawa Laboratory, with a longer-term interest in applied AI, technical solutions and AI-enabled scientific workflows.
